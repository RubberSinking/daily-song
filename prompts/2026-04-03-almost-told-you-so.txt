Original acoustic pop song with female vocals about quiet personal hope — holding a private wish close, protecting it from disappointment, the tender tension between longing and reality. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft brushed drums, walking bass, occasional piano shimmer. Around 90 BPM. Verse-chorus structure. Warm, understated 1990s-inspired production. Lyrics use everyday imagery: morning light, a cup of tea, ordinary moments that hold unspoken feeling. Chorus is warm and hopeful. Bridge is quiet and confessional.
