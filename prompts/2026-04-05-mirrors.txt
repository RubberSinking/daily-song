Write an original song in the style of tender, melancholy singer-songwriter pop. The arrangement centers on a close, emotionally revealing female lead vocal—intimate and vulnerable, as if confiding something private. Instrumentation is built from acoustic guitar as the emotional spine, with restrained bass, sparse or absent drums, and only soft electric guitar or keyboard textures added where they deepen the ache rather than fill space. The melodic contour feels inward and slightly descending in places, as though observing a quiet unraveling from up close rather than dramatizing it from a distance. The song captures emotional strain, loneliness, and private collapse through concrete, specific imagery and compassionate observation—avoiding clichés in favor of intimate detail. The chorus expands slightly but remains fragile, never shouting or crescendoing into relief; emotional intensity comes from honesty and tenderness rather than volume or release. Production should feel dimly lit, sympathetic, and human—like a diary entry that has been carefully shaped into a beautiful pop song. Tempo is moderate and unrushed. The overall mood is introspective, aching, and deeply personal.
